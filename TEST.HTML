Help & Troubleshooting

                
            

            

                
This app uses Web Serial/WebUSB and works best in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on desktop. It may also work on Android devices with a USB-OTG adapter. **It does not work on iOS/Safari.**

                
Device Not Found?

                
    
                    
  • Check your USB cable.
    • 
                    
  • Make sure you have drivers installed (if on Windows).
    • 
                    
  • Close any other apps that might be using the serial port (like Arduino IDE).
    • 
                    
  • Try pressing the **BOOT** and **EN** buttons on your ESP32 board while trying to connect.
